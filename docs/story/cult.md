# The Pale Choir

## What they believe

The Choir believes the kingdom should be ruled through dreams. Waking rulers are vain and
corruptible; a sleeping people, guided by voices in the night, would be orderly and
content. They worship **the Sleeper**, a Pokémon sealed for centuries beneath the
cathedral in Crownspire, and call its nightmares "the Hymn".

Their symbol is a **pale bell**: a white bell with no clapper. Members wear it hidden, as
a pin, a seal ring, or a stitched lining.

## What they want

1. Keep King Aldric in a nightmare so the Chancellor rules in his name.
2. Set the heirs against each other so that whoever wins the succession is weak,
   compromised, or theirs.
3. Finish the **Bell Tower's resonance engine** in Crownspire, which will carry the
   Sleeper's nightmares to every town at once. On the night it rings, the whole kingdom
   sleeps and the Choir governs every dream.

## Structure

| Rank | Who | Game role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hierophant** | Chancellor Maelis Venn | Final human boss (Champion slot) |
| **The Four Voices** | The king's Privy Council: Lady Orla Grise (Ghost), Master Dunstan Pell (Poison), Justiciar Hale (Steel), Sister Noor (Fairy) | Elite Four replacement |
| **Wardens** | Regional leaders, one per act: Silas Crane (Gilded Scale), Warden Mott (navy), Warden Ysolde (academy), Warden Kael (army) | Admin-level fights, the "Team Aqua/Magma admin" slot |
| **Acolytes** | Robed, masked foot soldiers; in public, Gilded Scale clerks and guards | Grunt trainers |
| **The Gilded Scale** | The merchant guild front | Shops, quest givers, money |

The Four Voices' types are chosen to fill the gaps the sibling Trials leave. Balance may
change them.

## Legendaries

Six legendaries, three on each side. The Choir binds theirs through fear and the bell;
the player's answer because of who the player is and what their mother kept.

### The Choir's three

| Pokémon | Role | Where it appears |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Darkrai** | **The Sleeper.** Sealed for centuries beneath Crownspire's cathedral. Its nightmares hold King Aldric asleep and seep into every town as bad dreams. | Felt from Act 1 (dreams), seen in the Act 2 Midsummer Court, fought in the undercroft in Act 4 |
| **Yveltal** | **The Choir's weapon.** Bound by Warden Ysolde with a dark relic. Drains life from what it passes over. | Abducts Seraphine at the end of Act 2; leads the attack in the Siege of Lowmere in Act 3; fought at the siege's end and driven off |
| **Giratina** | **The Hierophant's own.** Venn draws it out of the Distortion World by ringing the Bell Tower's great bell. | Final battle of Act 4, at the top of the Bell Tower |

Balance and features decide which are catchable, which are story partners, and their
levels. Draft assumption: the player can catch all three of theirs, and the Choir's three
can be caught in the post-game.

## Reveal beats

The player should suspect the cult well before anyone names it. Each act adds one
certainty.

1. **Act 1 (Trials 1-3): the bell.** The pale bell appears on Silas Crane's seal in
   Haymarket, on Thornfield's debt ledgers, and on a crate in the Cragholt mine collapse.
   Townsfolk mention bad dreams. No one says "cult". The player only knows that the
   Gilded Scale is behind three towns' troubles.
2. **Act 2 (Trials 4-6): the name.** Saltmere's night shipments are Acolytes. The player
   hears the name "the Pale Choir" for the first time. Seraphine explains the Sleeper,
   the king's illness and Elena's murder, then is abducted by Yveltal. The Chancellor declares a
   regency after the king collapses at the Midsummer Court.
3. **Act 3 (Trials 7-8): the hand.** Garrick's martial law is the Choir's doing through
   Venn's advice. Vespera's double-agent reveal confirms the Choir sits inside the
   Privy Council. The player learns the Bell Tower is the weapon.
4. **Act 4 (Trial 9 and Crownspire): the face.** Aurelian confesses. The Four Voices are
   unmasked as the Privy Council. Venn is revealed as Hierophant at the top of the Bell
   Tower, fights the player, then rings the bell and calls Giratina.

## Rules for writing the Choir

- They never rant. They are calm, polite and sure they are being kind.
- Acolytes speak in hushed, sleepy phrases: "Rest now." "It's late, little prince."
- Venn should be likeable for most of the game. He is the one courtier who is
  consistently polite to the player, which is exactly how he keeps them close.
