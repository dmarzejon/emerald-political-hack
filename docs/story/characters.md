# Characters

## The player

**Prince or Princess Rowan of Lowmere** (boy or girl and name chosen by the player;
*Rowan* is the default). 16 years old, the tenth and youngest child of King Aldric III and
the only one by Elena Marsh. The court calls them "the Marsh Prince" or "the Marsh
Princess", or simply "the bastard". Raised in Lowmere by their mother's friends after she
died when they were four.

The player is quiet and fills in their voice through choices, but their defining trait is
fixed: they take the town nobody wanted seriously. Their goal at the start is small, to
keep Lowmere fed. It grows into proving their worth, then into tearing the corrupt court
down, and at the end into a choice between ruling and building something better than a
crown (see [acts.md](acts.md#ending)).

On arrival in Lowmere, Mother Hesk gives them their mother's **Silver Wing**, which they
carry without knowing what it is. Soon after, a courier brings their father's gift: a
choice of **Entei, Raikou or Suicune** (see
[vertical-slice.md](vertical-slice.md#5-the-kings-gift)).

## The nine siblings

Fought youngest to eldest, each in the throne hall of their own palace. **Every sibling
is corrupt.** None of them is a cartoon villain, but each has personally chosen to profit
from or cover up harm to their own people, and each case is how the player earns the
right to a Trial. "Stance" is how they treat the player at first meeting. "Arc" is where
they end up after losing.

Each sibling also holds a **signature legendary**, bestowed at their Apportionment (see
[world.md](world.md#the-apportionment)). It is the ace of their Trial team. Trial types
are fixed; balance owns the teams and levels, and should scale the early legendaries
down so Trial 1 stays fair.

### 9th child. Prince Corwin, Haymarket. Normal. Legendary: Type: Null.
- **Age** 19. Charming, vain, a born salesman. The most popular sibling with commoners
  because he throws fairs.
- **Stance:** open mockery. As the second-youngest, he has been the butt of every joke
  until the player arrived, and he is relieved to have someone lower than him.
- **Corruption:** the Gilded Scale pays for his fairs and his debts. In return he
  personally signed off on the light weights that rig Haymarket's grain scales, and on
  the fines for anyone who complains. He knows the villages that depend on the market,
  Lowmere most of all, go hungry for it. He is more greedy than cruel, and he tells
  himself it's just business.
- **Legendary:** Type: Null, a masked, unfinished-looking legendary he treats as a
  showpiece. Fits a sibling who is all front: a legendary that hasn't come into its own.
- **Arc:** humiliated in the first Trial. Sulks, then slowly becomes the player's first
  ally among the siblings. In Act 3 he uses his market contacts to smuggle food to
  Lowmere during the siege. The comic-relief sibling who turns out braver than expected.
- **Palace flavour:** the Gilt Pavilion, a palace built like an auction house.

### 8th child. Princess Isolde, Thornfield. Grass. Legendary: Shaymin.
- **Age** 20. Gentle-seeming, idealistic, writes poetry about the land.
- **Stance:** pity. She is kind to the player in a way that makes it clear she thinks
  they'll fail.
- **Corruption:** her steward binds Thornfield's tenant farmers in debt, and Isolde
  signs every foreclosure herself. The land she seizes becomes her pleasure gardens and
  flower terraces. She calls it "returning the land to beauty" and does not visit the
  families she evicts.
- **Legendary:** Shaymin, which blooms in her terraces. Townsfolk say it only flowers
  where land has been taken.
- **Arc:** shaken when the case shows her the evicted families. After the Trial she
  returns the land, partly from shame and partly because the pact forces her to. Becomes
  a sincere but prickly ally; opens the sealed grove to the player in Act 2. Her town's
  pact sends seeds and gardeners to Lowmere.

### 7th child. Prince Brannoc, Cragholt. Rock. Legendary: Regirock.
- **Age** 22. Blunt, hard-working, proud. Works in the mines beside his miners.
- **Stance:** contempt for weakness, not birth. "Prove you can lift something."
- **Corruption:** to beat the Chancellery's ore quotas and earn its bonuses, he buys
  convicts and debtors from other towns and works them in the deepest, most dangerous
  pits. He has hidden the deaths from the magistrate for two years.
- **Legendary:** Regirock, which he says he dug out of the mountain himself.
- **Arc:** the Act 1 mine collapse was the Choir's warning to him when he started to balk
  at a new quota rise; he blamed the workers. After the Trial he respects the player,
  frees the bonded workers and defies the quotas for good. Becomes a steady ally, trying
  to earn back what he did, and brings his miners to the Siege of Lowmere and the
  storming of Crownspire.

### 6th child. Princess Marisol, Saltmere. Water. Legendary: Tapu Fini.
- **Age** 24. Sharp, witty, pragmatic. Runs a free port and plays every side.
- **Stance:** transactional. She'll deal with the player if they're useful.
- **Corruption:** she runs Saltmere's smuggling herself, including the Choir's night
  shipments, and sells passage to desperate families who end up in indentured work
  overseas. She takes a cut of everything and asks no questions.
- **Legendary:** Tapu Fini, the guardian of Saltmere's reef, which she keeps penned in
  the harbour as a status symbol.
- **Arc:** stays morally grey to the end. Sides with whoever is winning, which in Act 4
  is the player. Her navy blockades Crownspire's harbour at the climax.

### 5th child. Prince Teodor, Voltaine. Electric. Legendary: Regieleki.
- **Age** 26. Brilliant engineer, cold, obsessed with progress. Sees people as machines.
- **Stance:** indifference. He doesn't think about the player at all.
- **Corruption:** Voltaine's grid powers the kingdom, and Teodor cuts power to any town
  that falls behind on payments. Lowmere has been dark for a year. He also built the
  Bell Tower's resonance engine for the Chancellery for a fortune in gold, and chose not
  to ask what it was for. It amplifies the Sleeper's nightmares across the kingdom.
- **Legendary:** Regieleki, wired into Voltaine's main generator.
- **Arc:** when he learns what his machine does, he sabotages it in Act 4. Redeemed by
  action, not apology.

### 4th child. Princess Seraphine, Lumenhall. Psychic. Legendary: Uxie.
- **Age** 28. Scholar and seer, calm, unsettling. The only sibling who visited Elena.
- **Stance:** curious. She seems to know things about the player that they don't.
- **Corruption:** she has been investigating the Pale Choir for years, not to stop it but
  to take the Sleeper's power for herself. To keep her research secret she uses Uxie to
  erase the memories of students, servants and witnesses who learn too much. Some of
  them have never recovered.
- **Legendary:** Uxie, the being of knowledge, which she uses to wipe memories.
- **Arc:** the midpoint ally, and never fully trustworthy. After the Trial she tells the
  player about their mother, the Silver Wing and the grove where Xerneas sleeps, because
  she needs someone to break the Choir before she can take its place. The Choir abducts
  her at the end of Act 2, using Yveltal. Rescued in Act 4, she gives up her ambitions
  only when she sees what the Sleeper has done to the king.

### 3rd child. Prince Garrick, Emberfort. Fire. Legendary: Heatran.
- **Age** 31. General of the royal army. Believes order is everything.
- **Stance:** open hostility. He sees the player's campaign as sedition.
- **Corruption:** he sells officers' commissions to noble families, lets his garrisons
  extort "protection levies" from the towns they guard, and three years ago ordered
  soldiers to break up a bread protest in Emberfort. People died. He had the records
  burned and the survivors conscripted. Chancellor Venn knows, and uses it to steer him.
- **Legendary:** Heatran, which lives in the volcano beneath his forge-fortress and
  powers his armouries.
- **Arc:** Act 3 antagonist. Declares martial law when the king collapses and marches on
  Lowmere. Defeated in a Trial in the throne hall of his fortress palace. The case
  forces the massacre into the open. Surrenders his sword and lives with it; not a
  friend, but no longer an enemy.

### 2nd child. Princess Vespera, Duskmoor. Dark. Legendary: Chien-Pao.
- **Age** 33. The royal spymaster. Elegant, patient, unreadable.
- **Stance:** watchful. Sends the player anonymous help and anonymous threats.
- **Corruption:** she keeps files on every noble in the kingdom and blackmails half the
  court; she sells secrets to the highest bidder, foreign or domestic. She joined the
  Pale Choir as a double agent, but to replace Venn's network with her own, not to free
  anyone. She has done terrible things to stay trusted.
- **Legendary:** Chien-Pao, one of the Treasures of Ruin, bound to her by a stolen relic.
- **Arc:** the big Act 3 twist. She appears to betray the player to the Choir, then
  reveals she has been feeding them information all along (the anonymous letters),
  because the player is the only weapon that can break Venn. Still holds her palace
  Trial: she wants to know the player is strong enough before she risks her cover for
  them. Pays for it in Act 4, possibly with her life (D to decide).

### 1st child. Crown Prince Aurelian, Highcrest. Dragon. Legendary: Dialga.
- **Age** 36. The heir. Brilliant, beloved, gracious. Everyone's idea of a perfect king.
- **Stance:** courteous and unthreatened. He treats the player as a harmless younger
  sibling, which stings worse than insults.
- **Corruption:** he pays most of the kingdom's magistrates to secure his succession. He
  knows the king is being kept asleep and has let it continue because it guarantees his
  throne. He is not a cultist; he thinks he can control them once he is crowned.
- **Legendary:** Dialga, master of time. The heir who is content to wait.
- **Arc:** the final sibling Trial. After losing he confesses his complicity in front of
  the court. He can't undo it, but he stands with the player against the Choir.
  The lesson of the game in one man: good manners are not good rule.

## Lowmere

- **Bram Ashdown.** Retired royal Ranger, about 60, gruff, raised the player. Gives the
  starter. He was Elena's friend and knows more than he says.
- **Tamsin Reed.** 16, the blacksmith's daughter, the player's oldest friend. Loud,
  stubborn, impatient with nobles. The rival: she battles the player throughout, and
  becomes their envoy to the other towns. Replaces Emerald's May/Brendan rival role.
- **Mother Hesk.** Elderly herbalist, the town's memory. Keeps Elena's old house.
- **Reeve Abel Toft.** Lowmere's only official, a tired clerk. Tracks the town's
  rebuilding; the person the player reports to when a pact brings new resources.

## Court and cult

- **Chancellor Maelis Venn.** The king's chief minister for twenty years. Mild,
  precise, always reasonable. Secretly the Hierophant of the Pale Choir. See
  [cult.md](cult.md).
- **Silas Crane.** Guildmaster of the Gilded Scale in Haymarket, smooth and smiling.
  The first cult face the player meets, though he isn't revealed as one until later.
- **Elena Marsh (deceased).** The player's mother. Healer and the last keeper of Lugia,
  whose guardianship kept the Choir out of the king's dreams. Killed by the Choir; her
  death was recorded as a fever. Lugia withdrew to the deep sea the same night.
